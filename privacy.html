Privacy Policy

1. Who we are

OvaFlow is a personal PCOS and menstrual cycle tracking companion. This policy explains what information we collect, how it is stored, and the rights you have over it.

2. Information we collect

  • Account data: first name, last name, email address, and an encrypted password.
  • Health data you enter: period dates and flow, symptoms, mood, energy, cervical mucus, weight and height, and PCOS assessment answers.
  • Derived data: cycle predictions, phase estimates, and PCOS risk scores generated from your entries.
  • Optional feedback: ratings and comments you voluntarily submit.

We do not collect location data, device contacts, or advertising identifiers.

3. How your data is stored

Your data is stored in an encrypted managed Postgres database hosted on our backend infrastructure. Row Level Security is enabled on every table. Passwords are hashed and never stored in plain text.

4. AI processing

Your PCOS assessment answers are sent to Google Gemini through a server-side gateway to generate an educational risk summary. Your name and email are not included in the prompt.

5. Sharing

We do not sell your data or share it with advertisers or data brokers. Data is shared only with infrastructure providers required to operate the app.

6. Your rights

  • Access and edit entries from the Tracker and Calendar tabs.
  • Retake or update your PCOS assessment at any time.
  • Request full deletion of your account and associated data.

7. Data retention

Your data is retained for as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, associated data is permanently removed from the primary database within 30 days.

8. Medical disclaimer

OvaFlow is an educational tracking tool, not a medical device. Predictions and PCOS risk scores are informational only and are not a diagnosis.

9. Children

OvaFlow is not intended for users under 13.

10. Contact

Questions can be sent to ovaflow.support@gmail.com.
