OvaFlow Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 3, 2026

Important: Replace ovaflowsupport@gmail.com with your working support email before publishing this policy.

OvaFlow provides menstrual-cycle tracking, symptom logging, reminders, and educational PCOS risk information. This Privacy Policy explains what information OvaFlow may collect, how it is used, and the choices available to users.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on the features you use, OvaFlow may process:

  • Name, email address, authentication identifiers, and account metadata.
  • Menstrual-cycle dates, flow information, symptoms, notes, and reminder settings.
  • PCOS assessment responses and generated risk information.
  • Technical information needed to maintain a session, synchronize data, and diagnose errors.

2. Email, Google, and Apple Sign-In

You may sign in using email and password, Google, or Apple. When you use Google or Apple, that provider authenticates your account and may share information such as your name, email address, provider identifier, and profile metadata according to your selections and the provider’s policies. OvaFlow does not receive your Google or Apple account password.

Apple users may choose Hide My Email. In that case, Apple may provide a private relay email address instead of the user’s personal address.

3. How We Use Information

  • To create and maintain your account.
  • To display cycle estimates, symptom history, and personalized app content.
  • To save and synchronize entries across online and offline use.
  • To generate an educational PCOS risk assessment.
  • To operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve OvaFlow.

4. Offline Storage and Synchronization

OvaFlow may store session information, cycle entries, symptom entries, assessment status, reminders, and recently loaded information in app storage on your device. This allows portions of OvaFlow to work without internet access.

When internet connectivity returns, eligible locally queued changes may be synchronized with your OvaFlow cloud account. Removing the app may remove locally stored information from that device, but it does not automatically delete information that has already been synchronized.

5. AI Processing and Medical Disclaimer

Assessment responses may be sent to an AI service to generate an educational risk score and explanation. OvaFlow is not a medical device, and its outputs are not a diagnosis, treatment recommendation, or substitute for professional medical care.

6. Service Providers

OvaFlow uses third-party service providers to support authentication, cloud database storage, synchronization, app hosting, and AI processing. These providers process information as needed to perform their services and are governed by their own agreements and privacy practices.

7. Security

OvaFlow uses authentication controls, transport security, database access controls, and device app storage to reduce unauthorized access. No system can guarantee absolute security. Users should protect their devices with current software, a passcode, and other available device-security features.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Information may be retained while your account is active and as reasonably necessary to operate the app, resolve disputes, comply with legal obligations, and protect OvaFlow and its users.

To request deletion of your account and associated cloud data, contact ovaflowsupport@gmail.com. OvaFlow should add an in-app account-deletion control before public App Store release if account creation remains available.

9. Children’s Privacy

OvaFlow is not intended for children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. Users under the age of majority should review health and privacy decisions with a parent, guardian, or trusted adult when appropriate.

10. Your Choices

You may choose which optional information to enter, log out of your account, adjust device notification permissions, and request access, correction, or deletion by contacting OvaFlow.

11. Changes to This Policy

This policy may be updated as OvaFlow’s features or practices change. The effective date at the top will be revised when material changes are published.

12. Contact

Questions or privacy requests: ovaflowsupport@gmail.com
